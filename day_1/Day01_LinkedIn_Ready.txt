Most Solidity devs think reentrancy only hits state-changing functions.
They're wrong.
And protocols have lost hundreds of millions proving it.

Here's how it works ⬇️

𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗦𝗲𝘁𝘂𝗽:

No state changes. Just math. Looks safe, right?

𝗡𝗼𝗽𝗲.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗞𝗶𝗹𝗹𝗲𝗿 𝗣𝗮𝘁𝘁𝗲𝗿𝗻:

The view function returned 𝗰𝗼𝗿𝗿𝗲𝗰𝘁 𝗺𝗮𝘁𝗵 on 𝗶𝗻𝗰𝗼𝗻𝘀𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝘁𝗲.
